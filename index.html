About Me

I am a second-year Ph.D. student in Informatics at the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT), advised by Dr. Chenxi Yuan. My current research focuses on AI agent harness and AI in healthcare. Before joining NJIT, I received my M.S. degree in Electronic and Computer Engineering from Purdue University Northwest and my B.S. degree in Mechatronics from Tianjin University of Technology.

Research Interests

AI Agent Harness, Agent Self-Evolution, Reasoning and Memorization, AI in Healthcare
